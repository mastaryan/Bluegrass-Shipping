Competitive Rates & Pricing

Rates are designed to be competitive while ensuring reliable service. We offer tiered options with minimums to cover local runs efficiently. All prices subject to distance, urgency, and specifics. Fuel surcharges may apply and will be quoted upfront.

Bronze Local Same Day Service

Perfect for standard deliveries in Laurel County and surrounding areas.

Flat Zone Minimums:
- Within 10 miles: $25 flat

- Up to 50 miles: Discounted rates available
